Bulk Production Methods (All 11 Products)

Khushali Chemicals Jaipur ke official bulk manufacturing methods. Sabhi products ko weighing scale par tolein aur exact ratios follow karein.

Showing All 11 Bulk Methods
#01
Glass & Surface

GC - Glass Cleaner (Colin Quality)

Ratio 1:80 to 1:100 0 TDS Battery/DM Water Overnight Cover

Ingredients & Weights:

Method 1 (Standard): 1 KG Glass Cleaner Concentrate + 99 KG DM/Battery Water (0 TDS).
Method 2 (Best Result/Premium): 1 KG Concentrate + 97 KG DM/Battery Water + 2 KG IPA (Isopropyl Alcohol/Sanitizer).

Manufacturing Method:

  1. DM/Battery Water (0 TDS) ko weighing scale par tolkar container mein lein.
  2. 1 KG Glass Cleaner Concentrate daal kar homogenously mix karein. Agar IPA method use kar rahe hain toh saath mein 2 KG IPA bhi mix karein.
  3. Achhi tarah milane ke baad material ko cover karke overnight (raat bhar) aise hi chhod dein.
  4. Agle din crystal clear Colin quality glass cleaner ready milega, jise 500ml trigger spray bottles mein pack karein.
Pro Tip: Glass cleaner ke liye kabhi saada RO paani use na karein, usse kaanch par dhabbe (streaks) pad sakte hain. Hamesha 0 TDS DM water hi use karein.
#02
Toilet Care

TC - Diamond Toilet Cleaner (Harpic Quality)

Ratio 1:7 RO Water < 70 TDS 24 Hours Resting

Ingredients & Weights:

Concentrate: 1 KG Toilet Cleaner Concentrate
Water: 7 KG RO Water (jo ki 70 TDS ya usse kam ho)

Manufacturing Method:

  1. Weighing scale par tolkar 7 KG RO water lein (< 70 TDS).
  2. Isme 1 KG Toilet Cleaner Concentrate daalein aur achhi tarah homogenously mix karein.
  3. Mix karne ke baad bartan ko cover karke 1 din (24 ghante) ke liye rakh dijiye.
  4. 24 ghante baad thick blue Harpic quality gel tayyar ho jayega. Harpic angular blue bottle mein bharkar quality check aur pack karein.
Pro Tip: Hamesha weighing scale se tolkar hi paani aur concentrate lein. Proper thickness Harpic bottle mein daalne ke baad hi accurate check hoti hai.
#03
Kitchen Wash

Dish Wash Gel (Vim Gel Quality)

Ratio 1:9 RO Water < 70 TDS 24 Hours Resting

Ingredients & Weights:

Concentrate: 1 KG Dishwash Concentrate
Water: 8.500 KG RO Water (< 70 TDS)
Salt: 500gm Industrial Salt + 500-1000g Power Booster (optional for extra grease cut)

Manufacturing Method:

  1. Ek container mein 8.500 KG RO water lein, usme 500g salt daal kar properly mix karein jisse 9 KG salty water mil jayega.
  2. Ab isme 1 KG Dishwash Concentrate daal kar homogeneously mix karein.
  3. Puri tarah milane ke baad container ko 24 ghante (1 din) ke liye chhod dein.
  4. Agle din micro-foam khatam ho jayega aur crystal clear thick gel ready milega, jise 500ml push-pull bottles mein pack karein.
Pro Tip: Namak viscosity (gaadha-pan) ke liye zaroori hai. Namak ko hamesha paani mein pehle poori tarah ghol lein, concentrate baad mein daalein.
#04
Floor & Hygiene

WPC - White / Colour Phenyl (Rose)

Ratio 1:30 Water TDS 150-250 only Immediate Full Packing

Ingredients & Weights:

Concentrate: 1 KG White / Colour (Rose) Phenyl Concentrate
Water: 30 KG Water (Dhyan rahe: TDS 150-250 ka hi hona chahiye)

Manufacturing Method:

  1. 1 KG Phenyl concentrate ko ek bade container/bartan mein nikaal lein.
  2. Ab isme dheere-dheere karke 30 KG tak paani milana hai (Direct ek saath paani na daalein).
  3. Pehle 200-400gm paani daal kar mix karein, fir 500gm mix karein, fir 1 Ltr paani add karke mix karein, is tarah dohrate huye 30 KG paani milayein.
  4. Phenyl ready hone par turant bottles mein pack karein. Bottle ko neck (gale) tak full bharna hai taaki air space na rahe.
Pro Tip: Phenyl ke liye 0 TDS paani use na karein. TDS 150-250 wala paani hone se milky white emulsion stable rehti hai aur separation nahi hota.

Rohit Sambhawani Ke 3 Golden Rules For 100% Quality:

1

Namak Hamesha Pehle Gholein

Industrial Salt gaadha-pan (viscosity) ke liye hota hai. Agar concentrate mein seedha namak dalenge toh lumps ban jayenge. Hamesha paani mein pehle poori tarah ghol lein, concentrate baad mein daalein.

2

24 Ghante Ka Resting Period

Handwash, Dishwash aur Fabric Wash ko banate hi pack na karein. Mixing ke dauran bane micro-foam ko settle hone mein 24 ghante lagte hain. Iske baad hi product transparent aur thick dikhega.

3

Paani Ka Sahi TDS Chunein

Glass Cleaner ke liye hamesha 0 TDS battery water lein (RO water se marks padte hain). Phenyl ke liye 150-250 TDS paani lein (emulsion stable rehti hai). Toilet/floor cleaner ke liye RO < 70 TDS paani lein.
